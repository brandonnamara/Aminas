Combat

The battle now speaks the same prismatic language as the island: crystal health bars that show the size of every hit, a turn indicator, element-coloured moves with clear states, and conditions you can tap to understand.

Health you can readSegmented crystal bars; a pale band holds what was just lost, then drains. Below 25% the panel turns red and says Critical.
Whose turnTurn number, "Your move" or who is acting, and who acts first, read from the game's own speed rule.
MovesElement gem and colour, power, and the cost in words ("Special · every 3"). Recharging moves show the turns left.
ConditionsBuffs ▲ and debuffs ▼ with turns left, including attack down and guarding, which were invisible before. Tap one for an explanation.

Victory and defeat

One result card for every win: where it happened, progress, rewards grouped by where they came from, then the party. Guardians get gold, the Island Boss gets the full spectrum, and defeat stays quiet.

Rewards and the rarity ladder

Every reward uses one tile and one card. Rarity is readable at a glance and the celebration grows with it, while common rewards stay calm.

Prismatic icons

52 voxel-crystal sprites with one outline and one light direction replace the thin line icons for every "thing" in the game. Control glyphs (close, back, plus) and the Dev 8 currency sprites were already clear and stay.

Special moments

A new Amina keeps the creature as the hero, with a quiet line showing its place in your collection. A new island gets its own spectrum card.

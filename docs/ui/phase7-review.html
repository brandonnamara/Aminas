The arena follows the turn

The interface and the arena now point at the same creatures, in the same colours. On your move, your Amina gets a cyan voxel ring, an outline in its element's colour and a battle-ready move. The foe carries rose corner brackets. Pressing a move closes the brackets on its target. While a move plays, whoever is acting is lit and the other is marked as the target, in the arena and on its panel.

Movement with personality

All 63 Aminas still share one rig, but each species now has a motion profile worked out from its body shape, stage, element, speed and personality. That gives 14 distinct styles across the roster: pouncers, slammers, spinners, floating casters and plain lungers, in playful, fierce, sly and calm temperaments. Each style has its own idle, battle-ready, attack, special, hit, heal, guard, victory and defeat.

Element attacks

Attacks are built from voxel cubes in the element's colours:

  • Fire throws an ember ball with a rising trail, and water arcs.
  • Light is a fast beam.
  • Earth lobs a heavy rock.
  • Wind corkscrews.
  • Resonance cycles the spectrum.

Super-effective hits get a short freeze-frame, a camera punch and a shockwave. Special moves gather a column of light while the camera leans in.

Ten arenas, ten atmospheres

Each arena has its own light and its own air (pollen, lantern dust, spores, bubbles, embers, wisps, fireflies, wind gusts, crystal glitter, falling leaves). Most also get soft light shafts, and every one has distant voxel silhouettes for depth.

Guardians and the Island Boss

Boss rooms keep their halos, runes and title cards. Each also gets a dimmer key light, a coloured rim and a pool of light under the boss. For the Island Boss the rim slowly walks the spectrum.

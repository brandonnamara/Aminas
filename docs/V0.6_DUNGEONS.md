# v0.6.0-dev — Dungeon systems, world lore and the Battle hub (development build)

**Status: development build. Production is untouched.** Built on the published v0.5.0-dev voxel-island build
(the dev preview). Save key unchanged; saves migrate from schema 2 to 3 automatically and a copy of the old save is kept.
Character models were not touched: every creature, boss and scene uses the existing portraits and 3D builds.

## 2. What changed

**Progression (`src/campaign-data.js`, `src/rules.js`)**
* `D.CAMPAIGN`: each island lists its ten dungeons, its Island Boss dungeon (the tenth) and the island it unlocks.
  Each dungeon has exactly ten battles (`battles[0..9]`; `battles[9]` is the boss) and four sections (1–3, 4–6, 7–9, boss)
  with a one-time first-clear reward and optional journal entry.
* Dungeons open in order (Dungeon N needs Dungeon N−1 cleared) plus their own requirement (e.g. the Reliquary Gate).
* Progress is kept **per battle**: a defeat ends the attempt and the next attempt starts at the battle that was lost.
  Nothing advances on a defeat. Leaving mid-dungeon keeps the place too. Replays of a cleared dungeon start at battle 1
  (or straight at the boss) and never pay one-time rewards again.
* States: locked, available, in progress, completed (shown on every dungeon row and on the PvE menu).
* Defeating the Island Boss (battle 10 of Dungeon 10) the first time records it in `st.campaign.bosses` and unlocks the
  next island exactly once (`World.unlockByBoss`). `World.unlockIsland` refuses without that boss.
* Starter Island has all ten dungeons playable: 1 The Forgotten Road (new, full story), 2 The Gathering Hall (new,
  narrative direction + draft scene), 3 Overgrown Reliquary, 4 The Drowned Steps, 5 The Cold Kiln, 6 Hollow Veil,
  7 The Lantern Orchard, 8 The Windworn Stair, 9 Resonance Scar, 10 The Heartwood (Island Boss). Dungeons 4, 5, 7, 8 have
  names, themes, encounters, bosses and a one-line remark from the Entity, and deliberately no lore yet.
  Verdant Wilds, Obsidian Reach and Celestial Crown have no dungeons yet and say so honestly on the Island Map.

**Combat**
* Teams of foes: when one faints the next steps in (`foeIn` event). Used by 5v5 practice and raids.
* **The Waywarden** (Dungeon 1 boss, uses the Graniteling model at 1.45×): at 66% and 33% health it announces, then
  raises a **Stone Barrier** that soaks damage. Special abilities (any with a cooldown) crack it twice as fast; the
  ability buttons say "cracks the barrier", the telegraph banner explains it, and the barrier shows as its own bar.
* **Island Boss** (The Heartwood Stag, Briarstag model at 1.75×): prismatic glow (2D: animated conic aura and hue
  shift; 3D: a colour-cycling halo, colour changed in place each frame, no allocations), **Prism Shift** (its element
  changes every three turns, announced a turn ahead with what beats the next element), and a telegraphed
  **Prismfall** charge (never resisted, a guard blocks most of it). Labelled "Island Boss".
* New bosses for Dungeons 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 use the existing telegraph system with new moves (Lights Out, Undertow,
  Kiln Flare, Lamp Glare, Gale Force).
* Actions after a battle has ended are ignored; resolving an unfinished battle does nothing.

**Story**
* `src/scenes.js`: scenes as data (speaker, mood, background, creature on stage, Entity in/out, pauses, player choices
  with branches, labels). `src/ui-scene.js` plays them with typing, tap to advance, **Auto** (fast-forward) and **Skip**.
  Completion is stored (`st.story.scenes[id]` = seen / skipped). Scenes never grant anything: rewards and unlocks are
  applied by the battle result first, so skipping or closing the app mid-scene cannot lose or duplicate progress.
  A scene that was due when the app closed plays the next time the island opens.
* **Old Roads** (after the Waywarden): the agreed dialogue, the player's line as a response button, the mural
  close-up with the Entity's silence and looking away. About 46 s at normal text speed.
* The Forgotten Road tells its story through the environment: new block-style backdrops (`src/dungeon-art.js`) per
  battle — overgrown flagstones (1–3), signposts, doorways with small steps and a carved lintel of tall and small figures
  walking together (4–6), a mural uncovered a little more each battle (7–9: the two starter species side by side), the
  standing-stone gate (10) — and a one-line clue on each encounter card.
* **The Empty Hall** (Dungeon 2, marked draft) and **The Heart of the Island** (Island Boss) scenes.
* Journal (from the Dungeons screen): lore entries found in dungeon sections and ruins, and replays of seen scenes.
* Canon guard (automated): no new text names the Emergence, the Entity's origin, why the sanctuary exists, or the
  presence; the starter species' past is shown, never explained.

**Battle hub (`src/ui-pve.js`)**
* Battle → **PvE**: Dungeons · Island Map · Explore (the existing explore areas) · Loot Ruins · Survival.
* Battle → **PvP**: Matchmaking 5v5 · Island Defence · Hide and Seek. Every PvP screen says online play is not connected.
* **Island Map**: the islands as tilted fragments joined by faint seams ("Every island in the sanctuary is a piece of
  somewhere else."), each with its real state: you are here / unlocked / Island Boss defeated / locked (name hidden,
  a hint, and "Defeat the Island Boss of …"). Opens from the HUD island pill as before.
* **Loot Ruins**: three sites on the starter island, each opened by a dungeon; searches pay random gold/food (sometimes a
  gem) with a 30–60 min cooldown (seconds with Quick timers); the first search of each finds an artefact (journal entry).
* **Survival**: party of three, endless waves, a Champion every fifth wave, a little healing between waves; pays per wave
  beaten when you stop or fall; one-time milestone gems at waves 5/10/15/20; best wave recorded. Never touches dungeons.

## 5. Save compatibility
Schema 2 → 3 migration: dungeon clears and claimed first-clear rewards carry over (old floors 1, 2 and the boss floor
map onto sections 1, 2 and 4; the new section 3 counts as claimed after a clear), a run in progress resumes at the
matching battle with its health, bought islands stay unlocked, new fields get defaults, v0.1 saves still migrate
(1 → 2 → 3), unknown runs are dropped safely, newer saves are refused. A copy of the old save is kept under
`<save key>-v2`.

## 6. Decisions for you
* **"World Resonance" vs "the Emergence".** The existing intro, arrival scene and Ask-the-Entity lines call the
  catastrophe "World Resonance". The new canon calls it the Emergence. New text avoids naming it at all; the old lines
  are unchanged. Say which name to keep and I'll make the old lines match.
* **Island purchases with gold are gone** for islands not yet unlocked (the brief: Island Bosses unlock islands). The
  Verdant Wilds quest now points at the Island Boss.
* **Older saves keep what they cleared.** A v0.5 player who had already cleared the Reliquary can carry on from
  Dungeon 4 without first clearing the two new dungeons before it.
* Dungeon 2's scene ("The Empty Hall") is a draft.

## 8. Launch and test
* Play: serve the folder (`npx http-server .` or any static server) and open it; or use the dev preview link.
* Developer panel (Settings → tap the version line 7 times): new "Next dungeon: go to its boss" and "mark cleared".
* Tests: `node tests/run-all.js` (the browser suite needs Playwright with Chromium).
* Merging into the repo: apply `aminas-v0.6.0-dev.patch` on the `voxel-island` branch (`git am` or `git apply -3`), add
  the new `src/*.js` files to the service-worker precache (`node tools/stamp.js`), then run the v0.5 suites too.
